Seamless Connectivity to Every Corner
of the City

Lawrence Hill offers exceptional accessibility for both drivers and public transit users. Ideally located near the
DVP and Highway 401, residents can reach North York, Markham, and Scarborough within minutes, making daily
commuting efficient and convenient.
The area is also well connected by TTC bus routes along Lawrence Avenue East and Victoria Park Avenue,
providing reliable city-wide transit coverage. A short drive brings you to Don Mills Station and Victoria Park
Station, offering easy access to Toronto’s subway network.

ENHANCE PRIVACY:

IMPROVED AIR QUALITY:

ELIMINATES VIBRATION:

LOWER INSURANCE EXPENSES:

Concrete construction provides a sound
transmission class (stc) of approximately 60,
offering more than twice the soundproofing of
a single-family wood structure with an average
stc of 27.

Lightweight floor structures are prone to
unwanted vibrations from exterior traffic and
upper floor activities. A concrete structure
eliminates those vibrations.

IMPROVED FIRE SAFETY:

Concrete is one of the most fire-resistant
materials. Fire-related damage to concretestructured buildings is 7 times less than that
of wood-structured buildings.

Built for Safety & Lasting
Value

No air circulation between units. Independent
forced air heating systems and no crossover
circulation between units provide better air
quality, reducing moisture and mildew from
the building structure.

Concrete structure buildings have lower
insurance rates than wood structure buildings.

SUPERIOR QUALITY:

Concrete is more resistant to moisture,
eliminating odors and other smells from the
building.

Lawrence Hill features a rare full concrete structure, along with premium soundproofing and fire-resistant materials, reflecting a
carefully engineered design approach. These modern construction methods significantly enhance safety compared to traditional
wood-frame buildings, offering improved resistance to fire, moisture, heat, and cold. They also support better energy efficiency and
environmental performance, promoting a more sustainable way of living.

Kingdom is a diversified corporation
active in both Vancouver and Toronto,

development, land acquisition, planning
approvals,
product

construction management, sales and
leasing operations, and property
management. Its portfolio covers both
residential and commercial sectors,
including completed and ongoing
projects that continue to bring vitality
and value to urban communities.
