100% Solid Cast Concrete Construction

Unlike common wood-frame townhomes built across the Greater Toronto Area, Lawrence Hill Urban Towns are constructed entirely with 100% solid cast reinforced concrete. This commercial-grade structural system delivers unmatched acoustic privacy, lifetime resilience, and significant energy efficiency.

Lawrence Hill Cast Concrete Solid Concrete
  • Acoustic Isolation: STC 60 rating (whisper quiet sound insulation)
  • Floor Rigidity: Solid slab with zero squeaks or footstep vibration
  • Fire Safety: 2-Hour non-combustible concrete structural barrier
  • Long-Term Value: Lower insurance premiums & superior asset retention
Standard Wood-Frame Construction Wood Studs
  • Acoustic Isolation: Low STC 27–34 (transfers footfall & voices)
